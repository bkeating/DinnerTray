# Changelog

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-17

- Keep active tray capture running while other browser tabs are being discovered.
- Space automatic YouTube refresh attempts at least a minute apart, bypass cached page resources on recovery, and wait for playable data before restoring position.
- Surface YouTube player error codes in the tray status and recovery log. These changes reduce recovery disruption; they do not guarantee resolution of YouTube-side playback rejections.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-17

- Adopted the official Woolly dinner-tray logo across the executable, idle tray icon, About dialog, and README.
- Added a desktop slingshot, a breakable wooden block pile, trajectory preview, reset, and close controls.
- Sheep can be flung with mouse momentum and follow gravity-driven arcs.
- Added projectile, collision, stack-stability, and monitor-width simulation checks.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-17

- Added Woolly, an original animated desktop sheep, with window-edge walking, dragging, falling, and dismissal controls.
- Separated creature artwork from movement to support future characters.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-11-17

- Expanded detection beyond YouTube to compatible HTML video players.
- Added visible messages for unavailable protected frames and capture restrictions.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-17

- Added limited recovery for transient YouTube playback errors and restoration of playback position.
- Preserved tab tracking through player errors and reloads.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-17

- Initial Windows tray-video app with click-to-return, play/pause, a dedicated browser profile, and About dialog.

These versions describe development milestones. A corresponding GitHub release may not exist for every entry.
